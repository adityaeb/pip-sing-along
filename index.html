About the Pip Kids Uploader app

"Pip Kids Uploader" is a small private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to the Pip Sing-Along channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's YouTube account or data.
