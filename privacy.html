Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the "Pip Kids Uploader" application ("the App") and the Pip Sing-Along website handle information. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the Pip Sing-Along YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the App accesses

  • Google account authorization. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App receives an OAuth access token and refresh token with two permissions: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading basic channel information (youtube.readonly), such as the channel name and the privacy status of our own uploads.
  • Our own content. The App reads video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags that we created, and sends them to YouTube when we upload.

The App does not access, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users, and it does not access any Google account other than the channel owner's.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are used only to upload and manage videos on the Pip Sing-Along channel.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used by the App or by this website.

Deleting data and revoking access

The stored tokens can be deleted at any time by removing them from the owner's computer. Access can also be revoked through the Google security settings page (Third-party connections). After revocation the App can no longer access the account.

Children's privacy

Pip Sing-Along publishes content made for children, and its videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is used only by the adult channel owner and never interacts with children or viewers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
